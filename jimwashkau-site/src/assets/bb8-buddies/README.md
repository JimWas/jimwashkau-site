# Approved Little Mars Buddies

Character concept sheet approved by the user; transparent sprites generated with the built-in image_gen tool from that approved sheet.

- percy.png: Perseverance / Percy, cream and orange six-wheel camera-mast rover.
- oppy.png: Opportunity / Oppy, gold rover with dark-blue solar panels.
- ginny.png: Ingenuity / Ginny, gold helicopter with two coaxial rotor levels and four landing legs.

Generation prompt set: Extract only the corresponding large portrait from the approved design sheet into a single isolated full-body character sprite on a true transparent background. Preserve approved proportions, eye expression, smile, blush, colors, camera angle, shading and mechanical parts. Remove other characters, pose sketches, labels, background, ground, dust, shadows and decorative marks. No redesign, text, duplicates or cropping of wheels, antennae, arm, rotors or legs.

Sprites are used in SVG nursery scenes (including night vision and embedded snapshot exports) and buddy invitation cards.
